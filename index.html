Questions grown-ups ask

Does it fit my child’s current backpack?

Nested is designed as an insert for the bag your child already uses. Sizing details are still being finalized, so tell us your bag in the form below and we’ll keep it in mind.

Can I order it now?

Not yet. Nested is in development. The form below is an interest list. No payment is taken and it isn’t an order.

What ages is it for?

Kids ages 5 to 12, the in-between years when children start managing more of their own things.

How do I clean it?

The insulated base is designed to collect sticky liquids and crumbs so cleanup is fast. Final care instructions will come with the finished product.
